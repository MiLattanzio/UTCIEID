Hello OpenStore maintainers,

I am preparing UTCIEID (utcieid.milattanzio), an independent open-source Italian
electronic identity card client for Ubuntu Touch 24.04-2.x ARM64.

Source and build instructions: https://github.com/MiLattanzio/UTCIEID
Build: ./scripts/build-click.sh
Dependencies and pinned Avalonia source revision are documented in README.md.
Tests: dotnet test tests/UTCie.Tests/UTCie.Tests.csproj -c Release
Privacy: docs/privacy.md; permission details: docs/keyring.md.

The normal Click requests networking, nfc and camera. It supports CIE level-3
authentication with QR, physical NFC card and OTP, tested through a real INPS login.
Camera QR scanning and autofocus have also been verified on a Fairphone 5.

The full application stores the user's chosen full CIE PIN, authentication
certificate and optionally imported document portrait in the system Secret Service
login keyring. The CIE private key never leaves the card. We do not store PINs in
plaintext files, send them to our own server, access fingerprint data or record camera frames.

This needs extra session D-Bus permission for org.freedesktop.Secret.* and related
Properties/Introspectable calls on /org/freedesktop/secrets{,/**}, restricted to the
service peer label. The exact tested rules are in packaging/secret-service.apparmor.rules.
On the development device only this app's profile was extended, remaining in enforce.
The current Click does not install that manual policy modification on other devices.
Secret Service itself does not isolate credentials by calling app; this limitation
is explicitly documented, and our code addresses only UTCIEID-owned attributes.

Could you advise on an accepted way to package this scoped Secret Service permission
and review the full application? The standard policy groups do not expose Secret
Service. We have not changed the app to an unconfined template or bypassed review.

The store screenshots are generated from the real AXAML with synthetic fixtures,
marked DEMO, and contain no personal identity data.

Thank you.
